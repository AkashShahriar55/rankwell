# Conversion

Turning impressions into downloads: icon, product page, custom product pages, product page optimization.

## cvr-search-card: Most decisions happen in search results
A searcher sees the icon, name, subtitle, rating and the first screenshots or preview (or the search
results asset on iOS 27) and often installs without opening the page. Optimise that card first.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/

## cvr-icon: An icon that reads at small size
A simple shape, one idea, strong contrast, recognisable at 60 px and different from the icons next to it in
search results. The 1024 x 1024 App Store icon must have no transparency or alpha channel. Icons are worth
testing: they change conversion more than most text changes.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/ ; practitioner reports

## cvr-measure: Measure conversion per source
Conversion = first downloads / unique impressions, read separately for search and browse. Searches for your
brand convert far higher than generic ones, so a rise in generic traffic can lower the average while
downloads grow. Compare downloads too, not only the rate.
- Source: practitioner consensus

## cvr-ppo: Product page optimization (A/B test)
Test the icon, screenshots and previews of the default page: up to three treatments against the original,
one test at a time, for up to 90 days. Assets need review; alternate icons must be in the app binary. Wait
until App Store Connect reports a result with at least 90% confidence, and change few things per treatment
so the cause is clear.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/

## cvr-cpp: Custom product pages for different intents
Up to 70 extra product pages, each with its own screenshots, previews and promotional text and its own URL.
Pages can be assigned keywords so they appear in search for those terms instead of the default page; each
keyword combination belongs to one page only. Make one page per audience or use case.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/

## cvr-cpp-deep-link: Send custom page visitors to the matching screen
A custom product page can carry a deep link so the app opens on the screen the page promised. The app must
handle that link (universal links via Associated Domains are the robust choice), or the promise breaks on
first open.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/

## cvr-ratings-visible: Ratings are part of the card
The star average and count show in search results, so rating work (ratings-reviews.md) is conversion work.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/

## cvr-consistency: One story across icon, header and screenshots
Use the same colours, type and tone across the icon, product page header, screenshots and search asset so
they read as one product.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/
