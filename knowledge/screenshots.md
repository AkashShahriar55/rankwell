# Screenshots and previews

The images and videos on the product page and in search results.

## shot-first-three: The first three screenshots sell the app
In search results, portrait apps show the first three screenshots (fewer when there is a preview video or
the screenshots are landscape). Most people decide there without opening the page, so the first three
must carry the main benefit and what makes the app different. Never lead with a welcome or login screen.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/

## shot-one-message: One benefit per screenshot
Each screenshot makes one point with a short caption, large enough to read at thumbnail size. Show the
result the user gets, not a list of features.
- Source: practitioner consensus

## shot-real-ui: Show the app in use
Screenshots must show the app itself, not only title art, a splash or a login screen (Guideline 2.3.3).
Text overlays and device frames are fine. Use fictional data, not a real person's account (2.3.9).
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

## shot-caption-keywords: Echo the searched words in captions
When a caption repeats the words someone typed, they see at once that the app matches. Whether Apple reads
text in images for ranking is not confirmed; write captions for people.
- Source: practitioner consensus

## shot-specs: Sizes and counts
Up to 10 screenshots per device size, at least one. Provide the largest required iPhone size and, if the app
runs on iPad, the largest iPad size; App Store Connect scales them down for smaller devices. JPEG or PNG,
no transparency. Check the current required sizes before each release: Apple adds new devices.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/screenshot-specifications/

## shot-preview: App previews autoplay without sound
Up to three previews per localization, up to 30 seconds, made from footage of the app. They autoplay muted
in search and on the page, so the first seconds must work without sound; add short text. A preview is not
automatically better than screenshots: test it.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/

## shot-no-prices: No prices, 4+ content
No prices in screenshots or previews, and all images must suit a 4+ age rating even when the app is rated
higher (Guidelines 2.3.7 and 2.3.8). Mark items that need an extra purchase (2.3.2).
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/

## shot-creative-assets: Creative assets on iOS 27 and later
Since October 2026 an app can add creative assets: a product page header image or video above the
screenshots, and a search results asset that replaces the screenshots in search. They are reviewed in the
Asset Library on their own, without a new app version. The search asset should make the app's purpose
obvious at a glance and carry the main keyword in its text; the same content rules apply (no prices, URLs,
awards or other platforms).
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/app-store/asset-best-practices/ and
  https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/creative-assets-specifications/

## shot-test: Test before switching
Use product page optimization to test a new screenshot set against the current one. Polished designs do not
always win; the result decides (see conversion.md).
- Source: practitioner reports

## shot-localize: Localize captions per market
Translate captions and adapt units, dates and currency for each listing language. Screenshots stay in the
primary language where no localization exists.
- Source: Apple, https://developer.apple.com/localization/
